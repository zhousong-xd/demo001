## Learning goal

Teach **prop target choice**: with `calm_bell` stock **1**, at least **two** guests carry calmable conditions (`not_faces_unless` + `unless_state: calm`). The player must decide **whom** to calm — wrong target or early waste strands the table. Still only the six shipped rule kinds + existing L03 calm_bell prop pattern — **no new prop kinds**.

## Seats / guests / rules sketch

**Layout:** `6` → seats `A1 A2 A3 B1 B2 B3`.

**Guests:** all six (`fox`, `rabbit`, `crane`, `otter`, `tanuki`, `hedgehog`).  
**Props (existing only):** `calm_bell` stock **1**, `valid_targets` includes **at least two** calmable guests (sketch: `rabbit` **and** `tanuki`). Invalid / non-helpful targets: explain, **do not** consume (same L03 feedback pattern).

**Rules (existing six kinds + calm gating — this brief uses 5 kinds):**

| Sketch ID | kind | subject | other / seat / unless | Player-facing intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C07-r1 | `at` | fox | seat A2 | 轻锚，固定中列，逼对面竞争 |
| C07-r2 | `faces` | otter | other crane | 硬性对面：阿獭↔白先生（占一列） |
| C07-r3 | `end` | hedgehog | — | 端位，收窄剩余席 |
| C07-r4 | `same_row` | hedgehog | other fox | 小刺与阿狐同排 |
| C07-r5 | `not_faces_unless` | rabbit | other fox; `unless_state: calm` | 团团无安心时不能面对阿狐 |
| C07-r6 | `not_faces_unless` | tanuki | other fox; `unless_state: calm` | 狸掌柜无安心时也不能面对阿狐（第二可解除条件） |

- **Item target choice:** both rabbit and tanuki look calmable; stock is 1. Geometry + anchors should force **one** of the two conditional pairs into a faces conflict that only calm can clear, while the other pair remains avoidable by seating alone (exact forced pair = **另审** / TBD at JSON draft).
- Misdirection: calming the avoidable guest leaves the forced conflict intact → table stranded (reuse L03 “invalid/non-helpful” feedback; no new consume-on-miss).
- Calm only exempts that subject’s `not_faces_unless`; does not rewrite seats or other rules.
- Kinds **not** used here: `not_beside` (optional later). No second prop / dual tables.

**Geometry reminders:** faces = different row, same index; calm follows the **character**, not the chair (undo of calm restores stock + state — see C08).

## Tips outline (3 tiers)

1. 桌上有一枚安心铃，但看起来不止一位客人需要它。
2. 铃只能用一次；点错对象或太早用都会让剩下的矛盾解不开。
3. 先看哪一对对面是座位排不开的，再把铃给真正需要安心的那位。

(Aligned to #4 game-design baseline §13 #07 一枚铃够不够 — 道具的使用对象选择；至少两位有可解除条件，具体规则另审.)
