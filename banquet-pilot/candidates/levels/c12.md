## Learning goal

**Capstone exam (design only):** lightly mix **all six** shipped rule kinds plus the existing L03-style `calm_bell`, so the player shows understanding of adjacent vs faces, ends, same-row, conditional faces, and one calm target choice. Close on **comprehension**, not speed — **no** timer, life drain, or dexterity check (baseline §13 #12 — *以理解而非手速收尾*). Still **no new mechanics**.

**Layout:** `6` → seats `A1 A2 A3 B1 B2 B3`.

**Guests:** all six (`fox`, `rabbit`, `crane`, `otter`, `tanuki`, `hedgehog`).  
**Props (existing only):** `calm_bell` stock **1**, `valid_targets` sketch: **rabbit** (same L03 pattern; invalid targets explain and do **not** consume). Prefer a single calmable guest here (unlike C07’s dual-target teaching) so the capstone tests **when** to calm more than **whom**.

## Tips outline (3 tiers)

1. 这一桌像小考：六种关系都可能出现，还有一枚铃。
2. 不比手速；先分清相邻、对面、端位和同排。
3. 若团团带着铃铛图标的要求解不开，再考虑安心铃——用错对象不会扣库存。
