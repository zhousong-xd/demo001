## Learning goal

**Encourage try-and-undo:** the table should reward exploration without punishing wrong first placements. Players must feel free to swap, undo, and re-seat — **no** prescribed planner step order, **no** life/timer/paid-undo pressure. Still only the six shipped rule kinds — **no new undo mechanics** (reuse shipped atomic undo / history from product).

## Tips outline (3 tiers)

1. 这一桌可以大胆试，坐错了就撤回来。
2. 没有唯一规定的操作顺序；关系对了就算。
3. 若对面和同排缠在一起，先撤销最近几步，再换一组人试。

(Aligned to #4 game-design baseline §13 #08 撤回来也没关系 — 鼓励尝试与撤销；不强制按策划指定步骤解题.)
