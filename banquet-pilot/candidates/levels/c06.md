## Learning goal

Two **disjoint face-pairs** share one table axis: player must keep both across-table conversations without tangling the mental model. Visual relationship lines must stay readable (baseline §13 #06). Still only shipped `faces` + supporting kinds — **no calm required**, no new mechanics.

## Tips outline (3 tiers)

1. 这一桌有两组人都要隔桌说话。
2. 对面必须同列；两组尽量不要抢同一列。
3. 先落实阿狐那一列的对面，再给阿獭和白先生找另一列，最后安排端位与避开。
